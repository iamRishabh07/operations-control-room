---
name: interview-defense
description: Prepare a concise technical defense of the take-home design, Go code, SQL, failure modes and truthful AI use. Use before follow-up interview.
---

1. Ask one concise question at a time about product signal, assumptions, SQL, Go and React.
2. Demand a 20-35 second spoken answer followed by code evidence.
3. Give a small change or debugging scenario rooted in repository files.
4. Compare user's explanation with actual implementation; never fill gaps with invented design.
5. Note weak reasoning and an exact revision/drill.
